Exposure doesn't let the automatic collector open story pages, so most cards show only a cover photo. You can fill them in by hand: open a story in your own browser and click one button. Its photos are saved here, and you send them to the archive in batches.

01Add the button to your browser

Drag this yellow button onto your bookmarks bar. (Show the bar with Ctrl+Shift+B, or Cmd+Shift+B on a Mac.)

+ Add to archive

02Open a story and click the button

Pick a story below. It opens on Exposure. Click + Add to archive in your bookmarks bar, and this page reopens with the story ticked off. Stories already done are shown in green.
